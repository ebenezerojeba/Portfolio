How I work

Development process

Clear steps, written scope and working software at every milestone.

  1. Discover

    A conversation about your goals, users and constraints. I ask questions until I understand the problem, not just the feature list.

  2. Propose

    A written scope with features, milestones, technology choices and price. You know what you're getting before work starts.

  3. Build

    Development in milestones, with a working version you can click through and give feedback on at each step.

  4. Launch

    Testing, deployment, domain and analytics setup, and search engine foundations such as metadata and sitemaps.

  5. Support

    Fixes, improvements and new features after launch, under an arrangement that suits you.

Who is Ebenezer Ojeba?
